About

Benedetto Colella

I’m a photographer from Milwaukee, Wisconsin. My background is in commercial real estate and urban planning, and that’s the lens I’ve brought around the world over the past two years. Photography has been my creative medium to express how people move through and interact with the world around them. Beyond capturing iconic and obscure scenes, my photographs capture the energy of the places I go.
