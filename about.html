Portfolio Editorials

Benedetto Colella

I'm a self-taught photographer from Wisconsin. I first picked up a camera during the covid pandemic, when I was a bored student stuck at home. As I explored different styles and found my visual style, I noticed I was drawn to the built environment and the way people interact with it.

Once I finished university, I packed up my belongings and camera into a 40-Litre bag and set out to explore. I'm drawn to busy, chaotic cities, unique landscapes and the lesser-known regions of the world. My work spans landscapes, cities, architecture and street life across dozens of countries– but it's less of a travel archive than an ongoing study of the human environment.
