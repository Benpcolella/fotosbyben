## Research Tasks Before Writing Each Editorial

For each destination, research and document:

### Practical Information
- [ ] Exact region/province name
- [ ] Current population (metro vs city proper)
- [ ] Elevation (important for feel)
- [ ] Best months to visit
- [ ] Language(s) spoken
- [ ] Currency code
- [ ] Estimated daily budget (food, lodging, activities)
- [ ] Visa requirements (yes/no/duration)

### Key Sites & Monuments
- [ ] Primary historic landmark (the "Drum Tower equivalent")
- [ ] Secondary historic sites
- [ ] Religious/cultural sites
- [ ] Museums or cultural institutions
- [ ] Public spaces where daily life happens
- [ ] Natural features (parks, rivers, mountains)
- [ ] Modern architectural statement
- [ ] 1-2 additional unique spots

### Writing Research
- [ ] Brief history of the city
- [ ] Major cultural groups or influences
- [ ] Relationship to surrounding geography
- [ ] What the city is known for locally
- [ ] How it's changed in last 20 years
- [ ] What tensions exist (preservation vs modernity, etc)

## Image Organization Template

For each destination, organize images in the folder as:

```
editorials/destinations/[destination]/

REQUIRED:
- [Destination]_Cover.jpg (hero image for landing page)

ORGANIZE BY CATEGORY:
Historic/Monuments:
- [Landmark1].jpg
- [Landmark2].jpg

Streets/Daily Life:
- Street_Life_1.jpg
- Street_Life_2.jpg
- Vendor_Market.jpg

Architecture/Modern:
- Architecture_1.jpg
- Architecture_2.jpg

Nature/Landscape:
- Park_1.jpg
- River_or_Mountain.jpg

Museums/Cultural:
- Museum_1.jpg
- Temple_1.jpg
```

**Naming convention**: CapitalCase_with_Underscores.jpg
