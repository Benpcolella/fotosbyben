Notes from Chongqing

Issue 06

Mountain city shrouded in mist

Chongqing is a city that obscures and reveals simultaneously. Perpetually wrapped in mist and fog, it offers the photographer a natural filter—one that softens hard edges and creates an atmosphere of mystery. This isn't melancholy; it's atmospheric. The city exists in graduated tones of gray, shapes emerging and disappearing in moisture.

The geography here is dramatic. Built on mountains that plunge into the Yangtze River, Chongqing demands vertical thinking. Stairways lead to unexpected vistas. Alleyways open onto rivers. The city is in constant conversation with water and stone. Every corner offers a lesson in composition through constraint.

This is a city of grit and beauty—where ancient stone meets modern construction, where tradition persists in the smallest details, and where the mist reminds you that some mysteries are best left unresolved on film.
